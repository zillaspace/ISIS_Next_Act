Act one

A first run cinema,the home of Aspen Film,a gathering place for all.

A place the whole community comes to for entertainment, inspiration, connection and conversation. For over a century the Isis has been where Aspen sits down together to enjoy the art of cinema. The Next Act keeps that tradition and broadens it. The Isis continues to operate primarily as a cinema, showing first run films from major studio releases to award winning independent films, and Aspen Film curates a wide range of programming around them, including partnerships with other organisations that connect the community through screenings, special events and conversations. The renovated Isis, with its improved gathering and reception areas, gives that community programming, those special events and those collaborations more room to grow.

This year alone Aspen Film has partnered with the following organisations.

Act two

The road toopening night.

Dave Rybak of Rybak Architecture and Development leads the project as architect and project manager. The design is underway, and the goal is to move through the City of Aspen and break ground as quickly as the process allows.

  1. Summer 2026
    That’s a wrap
    Predesign

    Existing conditions, code analysis and the architectural programme.

  2. Fall 2026
    Now rolling
    Schematic design

    Plans, elevations and the material palette, priced by the contractor as we go.

  3. Winter 2026
    Design development

    Detailed drawings, then the Certificate of No Negative Effect from the City’s historic planning department, the gate before a building permit.

  4. Early 2027
    Permit and bidding

    Permit submittal, construction documents and contractor selection.

  5. Spring 2027
    Construction begins

    Ground breaks and the Isis starts its return to Aspen.

  6. 2028
    Opening night

    The architect estimates twelve months of construction. A spring 2027 start points to opening in the first half of 2028. Planning estimate, not a date from the architect

Act three

Every nameon one wall.

All gifts to the Next Act take their place on the Aspen Story Wall, in one of five circles.

A story installation rather than a donor list. Each name is set in bronze or stone with a page of its own: a line in the donor’s own words, and the Isis Theatre story they choose to tell. The favourite film they saw here. The afternoon out with their kids. The film that sparked the most conversation afterward. Part museum, part civic archive, and the record of the people who kept the lights on for the community.

1912

The year the story began.

The Isis opened in the same year Hollywood was being invented, in a town most people had already left.

1912

Universal Pictures and the studio that became Paramount were both founded. Aspen opened a movie house the same year.

Quiet

Aspen was deep in its quiet years after the silver crash. Building a theatre then was an act of faith in a town that had emptied out.

Isis

Isis was a major ancient Egyptian goddess of magic, healing, motherhood and fertility. The name predates the Egyptian craze that swept theatre design in the 1920s, which makes it an unusual survivor. Verify with Aspen Historical Society
